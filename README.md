⭐ Worth-It Score

One of the main features of DealScout AI is the Worth-It Score.

The product receives a score from 0 to 100 based on available information.

Example
<div align="center">
🟢 87 / 100
GREAT DEAL
</div>

Possible factors include:

Factor	Analysis
💰 Price	Current price compared with alternatives
⭐ Rating	Product rating
🛍️ Competition	Similar products
📈 History	Previous pricing
⚙️ Features	Product specifications
💵 Value	Features compared with price
💰 Price Comparison

DealScout AI presents different purchasing options in an easy-to-read format.

🏪 Store	💵 Price	📉 Difference	⭐ Rating
Store A	$999	—	⭐ 4.8
Store B	$949	🟢 -$50	⭐ 4.7
Store C	$1,029	🔴 +$30	⭐ 4.6

The best price can be highlighted automatically.

🛍️ Discover Better Alternatives

Sometimes the best deal isn't the product you originally searched for.

DealScout AI can recommend similar products that may provide better value.

Example
Product	Price	Rating	Savings
Product A	$999	⭐ 4.8	—
Product B	$799	⭐ 4.6	🟢 $200
Product C	$749	⭐ 4.5	🟢 $250

This allows users to make decisions based on value,not just brand names.

⚠️ Data Transparency

During development,DealScout AI may use demo product and pricing data when live shopping APIs are unavailable.

Demo information should always be clearly identified.

The application should never present fictional prices as real-time prices.

Future versions can connect to legitimate product and pricing APIs.

🌍 Long-Term Vision

DealScout AI is designed to become more than a basic price-comparison website.

The long-term vision is to create a personal shopping intelligence assistant that understands what users are looking for and helps them make better purchasing decisions.

Imagine searching:

“I need wireless headphones under $100 with good battery life.”

Instead of returning thousands of products,DealScout AI could analyze the available options and present the best choices based on:

💰 Price
⭐ Quality
🔋 Battery
🎧 Features
📈 Price history
🏷️ Discounts
🛍️ Alternatives

The result is a much simpler shopping experience.
